NextEdge, rebuilt in 4.5 hours

AI Product Development Hackathon · 20–21 August 2026 · Third of 15 teams · Team lead of 6

Our first idea was an app to document student council work, so councils don’t fade after the elections. After a faculty interview, I made the call to drop it. Few schools give students real decision power, so there was no market.

With 4.5 hours left, we switched to NextEdge: course builders sell verified courses in future-facing skills, and colleges pay to offer them to students.

I wrote the prompts and gave two teammates a part of the product each. I wrote the pitch content for another teammate to put into a brand tone, while someone else worked on cost and pricing. I managed all of them, designed the slides and the flow of the presentation, and debugged.

A returns process, redesigned as three workflows

AI Workflow Hackathon · 3–4 September 2026 · Winner of 14 teams · Team lead of 6

The case was improving a company’s returns management. We designed three connected workflows: one to map how returns work today, one to investigate and resolve each case, and one to find root causes, including suppliers, and stop returns repeating.

I built the architecture and the prompts, handed parts to teammates, debugged and made the key product decisions. I wrote the presentation script, gave everyone a role and coached three people on how to deliver it. I also scripted, shot and edited the promo video myself.
